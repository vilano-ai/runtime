# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

We want Vilano Runtime to be a technically serious and respectful project.

Participants are expected to:

- communicate respectfully and directly
- assume good faith
- focus criticism on ideas, code, and behavior
- avoid harassment, abuse, or discrimination

## Unacceptable Behavior

This includes:

- personal attacks
- harassment or intimidation
- hateful or discriminatory conduct
- doxxing or threats
- deliberately disruptive behavior

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, issues, pull requests, and other
contributions that violate this code of conduct.

Repeated or severe violations may lead to temporary or permanent exclusion from the project spaces.

## Reporting

If you need to report conduct issues, contact:

- `conduct@vilano.ai`
